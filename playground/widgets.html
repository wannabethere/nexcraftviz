1A compound tile

One card holding a headline with its delta, a multi-ring gauge, and the three counts the headline is made of. Vega-Lite cannot express this — the headline and the strip are card furniture around a chart, not marks inside it — which is the whole reason widgets exist alongside vconcat.

Two details that silently break this

  • startAngle belongs on the mark while the end angle is a theta encoding with scale: null. Setting both as mark properties validates, compiles, and draws nothing.
  • autosize: none. The default re-fits the view around the marks, which shifts explicitly positioned arcs off centre.

6The other way: one spec

When everything being combined is a Vega view, concat gives a single spec instead — one render, one PNG export, and the option of shared scales so panels can be read against each other. It cannot hold a KPI tile or a rich table, which is exactly when a widget is needed.
